Technical FAQ

Quick answers to common implementation and technical questions.

Is ITdose cloud-native or on-premise? expand_more
Adidose Health Systems offers a hybrid approach. While our platform is cloud-native for scalability and remote access, we also provide local-cloud deployments for institutions with strict data residency requirements.
What are the hardware requirements for the terminal clients? expand_more
Our system is designed to be lightweight. Any modern browser-capable machine with 4GB RAM can run the interface. For diagnostic imaging workstations, we recommend dedicated GPU support for HL7/DICOM rendering.
How does the system handle internet outages? expand_more
We use a robust offline-sync technology. Local critical patient data is cached and synchronized automatically once connectivity is restored, ensuring zero interruption in patient care.
Can we integrate our existing lab equipment? expand_more
Yes, Adidose supports standard HL7, DICOM, and proprietary protocols for most major medical equipment manufacturers including GE Healthcare, Siemens, and Roche.
